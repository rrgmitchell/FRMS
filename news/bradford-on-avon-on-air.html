Bradford-on-Avon RMS takes to the airwaves

While coronavirus has put our normal group meetings on hold for the foreseeable future, members of the Bradford-on-Avon RMS have not let this stop the music. Robin Benton, the group’s Chairman, with his wife, Lynne, have been asked to contribute a series of music talks for the new West Wilts Radio station. The talks take place on Sunday afternoons at 2pm and are called The Classical Music Hour. If you want to listen, the talks are available on the West Wilts Radio internet site through their Play Again facility.

This is undoubtedly a fabulous way to promote local societies and Federation members might want to consider the possibility of contacting their local radio station and offering a similar service. If you are interested, please contact the Federation Secretary at secretary@thefrms.co.uk for further information.
