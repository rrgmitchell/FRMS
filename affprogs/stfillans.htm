F For the appreciation of classical Classical music by residents of St. Fillans and Strathearn district, and to provide a Fund for the assistance of young musicians.

The St Fillans Music Circle is now preparing for its next term more ...

Members pay two half-yearly subscriptions of £45 at the start of each term, for which they receive eleven presentations including the light lunch, plus £1 for the Young Musicians' Fund, from which grants are made to promising and talented music students.

Alternatively, a supporter can pay at the door for individual attendances.
