The Society meets on Thursday evenings from early September until mid May
in the auditorium of the St Annes Road Baptist Church,
approximately 200 yards south of the
junction of Waterloo Road and St Annes Road.
(click for map)

The church is adjacent to the Fire Station and there are car parking facilities.

The church provides relaxed and comfortable surroundings for our informal and friendly meetings and is conveniently situated for communications including easy access to the bus services 5, 7 & 16.

Recitals are limited from 7:30pm until 10:00pm with a 20 minutes interval for refreshments.
