The Portslade Recorded Music Society is a lively and friendly Society,
where you are always guaranteed a warm welcome.

Frequency of Meetings?

We meet on the first Tuesday of each month
from September to May (not January) from 2pm to 4.30pm.

Where do we meet?

At the Business Centre, Loxdale English Centre
Locks Hill, Portslade
BN41 2LA
(see map)
Buses 1, 1X, 2 or 46 to Southern Cross
Free Car Parking
15 minutes walk from Portslade Station

Admission?

Members: £3.00
Non-members: £3.50
Annual subscription for membership: £3.50
Includes Tea/Coffee & Cakes/Biscuits in the interval

Interested?

Then contact the Society Treasurer on 07957 812614.

Or just come along!
