We meet fortnightly from September to May
at the Bedford Hotel in the middle of Tavistock (Above, left)

Our meetings start at 7.30 and aim to end at 9.45
Refreshments are available during the interval

As well as our own 'in house' presenters, we also have a number of
visitors who give presentations on specialised topics
(see the programme below).

Membership £12 p.a. Entry Fees £3 per member

Non-members: first attendance free, thereafter membership or £4.50 each attendance
