CROYDON RECORDED MUSIC SOCIETY

Meetings are held at St Mildred's Church Centre, Pavilion Room, Bingham Road, Croydon CR0 7EB

St. Mildred's Church Centre is located on the corner of Bingham and Sefton Roads
which can be accessed from Shirley, Lower Addiscombe and Addiscombe roads.

Meetings are held in the Pavilion Meeting room which is accessed via the side path next to the church.

We meet on 2nd & 4th Friday of each month except for August & December
and also March/April depending when Easter falls.

Meetings start at 7:45 with arrivals from 7:30 and finish at 9:35.

Annual subscription is £30 plus £4 recital fee per evening.
Visitor's fee is £5

Enquiries to Hon Secretary, Liz Brereton
