...meets on Friday afternoons at 2.30pm in the Mallinson Room of Wallington Public Library, Shotfield, Wallington.

Programmes are given by our own members, members of other music societies and people from the outside world of music. They start at 2.30 pm and last for about two hours with a break for refreshments.

Contact Hon Sec Liz Brereton on 07766 218514 or brereton59@gmail.com.
