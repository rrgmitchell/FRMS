Our meeting place

For location map press Community Centre

We meet in one of the buildings on the perimeter of Walton Community Centre,
with plenty of free parking adjacent.
As you travel from the Walton (Stone) roundabout north along the A34 dual
carriageway, Walton Community Centre is signposted, after a very short
distance, as a turning left into Whitemill Lane.
The Centre is on the left hand side of this road.
Your first visit is free. Come and see whether you enjoy the music.
We're sure you'll enjoy our company!
