In common with other societies we are unable to meet at present during the current covid 19 crisis.
It is our intention, once we are able to meet again, to resume where we left off.
Our last meeting was on 10th March, so the programme scheduled for April will now be
presented on the second Tuesday of whichever month we are able to meet again.

We will update this information here as details become available.

 

The JUBILEE MUSIC CIRCLE (Derby) invites you to come and enjoy listening
to good music in the company of other music lovers.


We meet on the second Tuesday afternoon in the month at the
Baptist Church Hall, Thornhill Road, Littleover
for informal programmes of recorded music.


The Jubilee Music Circle is a new name for Derby Music Circle, to mark
our change of venue and format in the Queen’s Jubilee year, 2012.

The name Derby Music Circle is retained for formal and legal purposes.
