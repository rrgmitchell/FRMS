RUSHDEN CLASSICAL MUSIC SOCIETY

Founded in 1962 as the Rushden and District Gramophone Society, and in 1987 changed its title to the Rushden Classical Music Society.
We are a small friendly group who meet every fortnight to share and enjoy classical music, our programmes including a wide variety of music from classical symphonies, concertos, operatic arias, choral and film music, songs from musicals, jazz, popular music and singers.

All meetings are suspended until further notice, owing to the Coronavirus, but we hope it won't last too long.


If you enjoy listening to music come and join us.

Meetings are from 7.30 p.m. in Hope Methodist Church, Linnets Lane, Higham Ferrers
