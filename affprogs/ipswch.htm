IPSWICH RECORDED MUSIC SOCIETY

71 st year


The Ipswich Recorded Music Society was formed in 1946 as the Ipswich Gramophone Circle to provide classical music on records to all of those who have an interest in listening to serious music of a wide variety in a fast changing world and in comfortable surroundings.

That is still our aim today and we welcome anyone who has similar views to join us in listening to music in a cordial spirit of music appreciation.

Our meetings run from September through to June.
We meet on alternate Thursdays
at
19 Tower Street, in Ipswich town centre.

Click for map

Meetings start at 7.30pm.

There is car parking at the rear of the building.
