The Society has been affiliated to the FRMS since 1947, although we believe that it existed before that date, and is committed to the shared enjoyment of classical music, broadly understood. Enthusiasm for classical music is more important than extensive knowledge. Visitors are always welcome to join us for an evening.

We meet on the first and third Fridays of each month. Meetings start at 7.30pm and end before 10.00pm.
