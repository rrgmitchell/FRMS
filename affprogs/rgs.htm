Ruislip Gramophone Society is a lively recorded music society which meets on selected Friday afernoons from September to May.

We meet in the Hardwick Room, Ickenham United Reform Church, Swakelys Road, Ickenham, Middlesex UB10 8BE from 2:30 pm–4:30 pm.

There is limited car parking on the church site, and it is accessible by bus from Ruislip and from London Transport rail services to Ickenham LT station.

The annual subscription is £40. Visitors are welcome as temporary members for a £3 donation.

To attend a meeting as a guest, or for further information, contact the Secretary Mike Paddy (01895 762561 or 07568 495584) or email mepaddy@live.co.uk.
