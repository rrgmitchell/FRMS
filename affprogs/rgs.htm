Ruislip Gramophone Society is a lively recorded music society which meets on alternate Friday afernoons, sometimes at the home of our Chairman, and other times at the home of our deputy Chairman Denise Hammond. There is limited street parking nearby.

The annual subscription is £20. Visitors are welcome as temporary members for a £1 donation.

To attend a meeting as a guest, or for further information, contact the Secretary Mike Paddy (01895 762561 or 07568 495584) or email mepaddy@live.co.uk.
