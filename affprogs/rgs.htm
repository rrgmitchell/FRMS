Ruislip Gramophone Society is a lively recorded music society which meets on alternate Tuesday evenings in Room 5 at the Methodist Church, Ickenham Road,Ruislip HA4 7BZ from 8.00 to 10.00pm. Limited parking is available at the church and there is street parking nearby.
The annual subscription is £30. Visitors are welcome as temporary members for a £3 donation which is refundable on becoming a member.

Contact the Secretary for further information:
