The Cirencester Music Society

Meetings of the Society are held at
the Ashcroft Centre,
Ashcroft Road,
Cirencester
and start promptly at 7.30 pm.

The Centre has a loop system and is wheelchair friendly.

There is a break for refreshments at about 8.30 pm and meetings end at approximately 9.30 pm.

During the season evening meetings are held on the 1st and 3rd Wednesdays of each month, except for September when we meet on the 3rd Wednesday only. We break after our second December meeting until March avoiding these winter months. Our season ends in June.

In addition, a programme of coach outings to concerts and musical events is run during the season with members benefiting from group booking terms and no parking problems.

Visitors and members' guests are always welcome at Society meetings, and concert outings numbers permitting.

The Committee welcomes suggestions for future programmes.

This is a friendly society for people of all ages who enjoy music and the company of others with similar tastes. The Committee wishes everyone an enjoyable season.

If you wish to join the Society or come as a guest then please contact our
