Mmeetings are held on MONDAY evenings

at 7.30pm (until 9.30pm) in

The Music Room, off St Ann Street, Salisbury.


Visitors and new members are very welcome. There is ample parking.

For details of the location of the venue, and other information about meetings and membership,
please telephone Ed Tinline 01722 328901


Refreshments are available during the interval.

Come and hear wonderful music played on superb equipment!

Membership: £20 p.a. Temporary Membership £3 per evening
