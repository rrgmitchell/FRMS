The society welcomes Full Members along with those only wishing to attend occasionally or perhaps as a guest of an existing member.


Current Fees:

Full year's membership = £40.00 per person (April – March).

Temporary membership per meeting = £5.00 per visitor or guest.

Cheques should be made payable to: W-S-M Music Appreciation Society.
Note: Fees are subject to review at the discretion of the committee.
