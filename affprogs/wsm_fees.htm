The society welcomes Full Members along with those only wishing to attend occasionally or perhaps as a guest of an existing member.


Current Fees:

Annual membership = £45.00 per person (from April).

Guests and visitors per meeting = £5.00 per person.

Payments can be made in cash, by cheque or bank transfer, which is the preferred option.
Cheques should be made payable to: WSM Music Appreciation Society.
Note: Fees are subject to review at the discretion of the committee.
