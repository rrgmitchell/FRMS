The Society meets at Wiltshire Music Centre,
Ashley Road,Bradford-on-Avon, Wiltshire

on the last Friday, each month,
from 19:30 - 21:30. ( September - June )

 

Bradford on Avon Recorded Music Society presents its 17th season of concerts of recorded music at the Wiltshire Music Centre, Ashley Road, Bradford an Avon. The aim of the Society is to present on excellent sound equipment a broad range of classical music on compact disc, selected from the whole of the recording era, circa 1900 to the present day.
Programmes include a wide variety of orchestral, chamber, instrumental and vocal music, chosen and presented by members and by visiting specialists, giving background about the music or performers.

We have a short informal refreshment interval when members can chat or discuss the music or other matters of interest with each other or the presenter. Visitors are very welcome at any of our meetings. Whether you come with a friend or alone, you will find the atmosphere friendly and welcoming.
