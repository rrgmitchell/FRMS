MUSIC PRESENTATIONS FOR 2022–2023 SEASON

Meetings begin at 7.30 pm, ending at approximately 9.45 pm, on alternate Friday evenings in a pleasant room at Redwood Manor, Tanners Lane, Haslemere, whilst our usual venue at St Hugh’s Education Centre, Holy Cross Hospital is unavailable due to hospital refurbishment. Our presentations of mainly classical music are given by Society members using a good quality hi-fi system.
