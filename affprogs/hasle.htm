MUSIC PRESENTATIONS FOR THE 2025–2026 SEASON

Meetings begin at 7.30 pm, ending at approximately 9.45 pm, on alternate Friday evenings in a comfortable room at Redwood Manor, Tanners Lane, Haslemere.

Our presentations of mainly classical music are given by Society members or guest presenters using a good quality hi-fi system.
