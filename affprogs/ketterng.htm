The Kettering & District Recorded Music Society was formed in 1958. Its purpose is the stimulation of and appreciation of programmes of recorded music, in a friendly atmosphere. Visiting members are always welcome to attend our meetings which take place on the second and fourth Wednesday of each month, at 7.30 to 10pm at the...

Corn Market Hall, London Road,Kettering

Annual Subscription £5.00, payable in October.
Members pay £3 for each meeting. Visiting members pay £3.50 for each visit (includes tea and biscuits).
