Weston-Super-Mare Music Appreciation Society - Programme


 

  Our spring/summer programme commenced on Wednesday 12th April 2023.  


2023/24 Autumn/Winter Programme:

2023  
November 8th ‘Gems from the World of Opera’ – Dr Andrew Borkovski, former lecturer at Bristol University
November 15th ‘What Passing Bells… Benjamin Britten’s War Requiem 60 years on’ – Geraint Lewis, Composer and presenter
December 13th ‘Hundreds and Hundreds’ – David Threasher, a sub-editor of ‘The Gramophone’
2024  
January 10th ‘English composers of the 19th & 20th centuries’ (A concert programme with audio and visual sources) – Tom Shearman, society member and retired professional musician/teacher
February 7th ‘Music of the Ukrainian diaspora in the UK; Past and Present’ – Dr Mariia Romanets, Honorary Research Associate – Bristol University
March 6th ‘Fourth Finger on G’ – John Hickley, society member
April Annual General Meeting (actual date to be announced)

 

Meetings are on Wednesday afternoons - 2:15 for a prompt 2:30 start

Please note—Dates may be cancelled or changed due to circumstances beyond our control.
