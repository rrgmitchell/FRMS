Weston-Super-Mare Music Appreciation Society - Programme


 

  Our spring to autumn programme 2024...  
10th April. AGM. Followed by a selection of short presentations by members.
24th April. ‘The Land Without Music - The British Musical Renaissance’.
Dr David Bednall, Composer, organist, pianist, and Cathedral Choral Director.
8th May. ‘Images in Sound.’
Michael Bass, Society member.
22nd May. ‘Guitarmageddon’
Bob Inskip, Member of Chard u3a.
5th June. ‘The Short but Creative Life of Samuel Coleridge-Taylor’
Dianne Board, Society member.
19th June. ‘Beyond the Planets’
Dr Christopher Redwood, Musician and author.
3rd July. ‘Summer Days: The Music of Eric Coates’.
Prof. John Pickard, Professor of composition at Bristol University.
31st July. ‘Purcell… England’s gem’
Owen James, Director of musicals.
21st Aug. ‘Sir Karl Jenkins Fourth Career.’
Jeff Castrey, Society member.
18th Sept. ‘Where I’m Coming From’
Jude Forth, Friend of the society and regular presenter.
9th Oct. Society Anniversary Celebration with tea and cake!
Gregory Steward, tenor, will entertain us.
6th Nov. ‘Jazz, Ancient and Modern - Has Jazz Always Existed?’
John Ogborne, Amateur musician.

 

Meetings are on Wednesday afternoons - 2:15 for a prompt 2:30 start

Please note—Dates may be cancelled or changed due to circumstances beyond our control.
