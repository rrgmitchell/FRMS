Weston-Super-Mare Music Appreciation Society - Programme

2023 Programme dates:

April 12th ‘Over the Pond’ – Mike Bass, Chairman
April 26th ‘Folk Influences’ – Chris Hughes, Street & Glastonbury RMG
May 10th ‘All at Sea’ – Bob Inskip, Ilminster RMS
May 24th ‘A Singer Who Sings No More - Dame Janet Baker’ – Margaret Hopes , Music teacher
June 7th ‘London - The Musical Capital of the World?’ – Geoff Trinick, Chairman of Cardiff RMS
June 21st ‘Hundreds and Hundreds’ – David Threasher, a Sub-editor of ‘The Gramophone’
July 5th ‘Monsieur Alkan is Never at Home; the life and music of 19th century’s most mysterious composer’ – Prof. John Pickard, Composer and Professor of Composition
July 26th ‘A Few Women of Note’ – Simon Rawlings, Vice-Chairman and FRMS committee
August 30th ‘The Devil’s Horn’ - The story of the Saxophone – George Young, society committee
September 20th ‘Gems from the World of Opera’ – Dr Andrew Borkovski, formerly at Bristol University
October 11th ‘How and why does Music make us Laugh and Cry?’ – John Ogborne
November 15th ‘What Passing Bells…’ Benjamin Britten’s War Requiem 60 years on – Geraint Lewis, Composer and a Sub-editor of ‘The Gramophone’
