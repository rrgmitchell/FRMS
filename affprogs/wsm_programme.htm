Weston-Super-Mare Music Appreciation Society - Programme


 

  Our 41st Season was due to start on April 15th 2020. However, it has been decided to POSTPONE the programme until further notice because of the current situation with regards to Coronavirus.

 

  

We normally meet on Wednesday evenings twice a month from April – September with additional social events both during and outside our normal season. However, the society will not be arranging any events for the time being. During this period, members will receive regular updates.
