Carnoustie
Recorded Music Society


Carnoustie Recorded Music meet in

The Newton Hall, Newton Panbride Church, Arbroath Road, Carnoustie, Angus DD7 6BL.

Meetings begin at 7 pm, ending around 9 pm. Temporary Membership £5.
Meetings take the form of musically illustrated talks.

Guests are always assured of a warm welcome.
Contact John Maidment, Hon Chairman, on 01241 243364
