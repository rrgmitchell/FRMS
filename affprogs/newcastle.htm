WHO ARE WE? - we are a group of people who share an interest in
the enjoyment of music, mainly classical.

OUR RECITALS - are mainly presented by our own members, or by
guests from other societies. They are a personal slant on some aspect
of the enjoyment of music. The cryptic titles are gateways to an evening
of enjoyable listening.

OUR MEETING PLACE - a pleasant room, well heated in winter, and
with cushioned seats for approximately 40 people.

OUR VENUE - We meet fortnightly on alternate Friday evenings from September to May
(with some adjustment to allow for seasonal dates and concerts at the Victoria Hall)
at the QUAKER MEETING HOUSE Miller Street, Newcastle ST5 1EF,
where there are excellent facilities and ample FREE parking at the rear.
(Miller Street is off King Street (A53) with the Victoria Inn on the corner)

AN INVITATION - if you are interested, please come along as our guest
for the evening. We can assure you of a friendly welcome, and if you
enjoy what we have to offer you might like to join us.

 

MEETINGS begin promptly at 7.30pm and end at 9.30pm.

MEMBERSHIP - Annual Subscription is £10 plus a recital fee of £3.00
which includes tea/coffee and biscuits during the interval.
