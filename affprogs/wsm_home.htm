Come and join a friendly group of people with a shared interest in a wide range of music.
 
  • Afternoon meetings at: Milton Methodist Church, 2 Milton Hill, Weston-Super-Mare BS22 9RA
    2:15pm for a prompt start at 2:30pm!

    (The hall is located behind the main church building.)

  • Professional presentations by guest speakers
  • Talks given by our members
  • Listen to an interesting and wide selection of music
  • Tea, coffee & biscuits provided during the interval
  • Non-members (visitors) are welcome

Although most of our meetings involve recorded music, we occasionally have presentations which include a ‘live’ performance.
