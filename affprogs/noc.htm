Norwich Opera Club

We are a friendly and informal gathering of opera enthusiasts and theatre-goers
who have been getting together every two weeks since 1970.
We do not produce live performances; we are a club for the appreciation of opera.

Meetings are held on alternate Thursdays at 7.00pm
in the St Peter Mancroft Octagon, Hay Hill, Norwich.

Click for map

Subscriptions are £30 per annum plus £5.00 per evening.

Your first visit is free!
Visitors are always welcome and pay £8 per evening after their first free taster.

We meet at 6.30 pm for half an hour to enjoy a glass of wine or other refreshment and to chat.
The opera begins at 7.00 pm and concludes at 9.30 pm.
