Committee Member Profile

VIce-President Tony Baines

A retired primary school headmaster, Tony was Federation Secretary from 2002 to 2011. He is presently Treasurer of the Stafford RMS.

Musically, he would retire to a desert island with J S Bach's complete works for solo violin and cello, and the complete works of Beethoven—and, his not so secret passion, the entire output of Hector Berlioz.
