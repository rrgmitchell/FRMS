Committee Member Profile

Vice-President Jim Bostwick

Jim was the FRMS Secretary from 2011–2018 and feels very honoured to recently have been made a Vice-President of the Federation.

He retired in 2004 after 34 years trying to teach 11–16-year-olds. Now he is an active cyclist (easy routes preferred), rambler, camper and gardener. Hobbies are anything to do with audio / HiFi (caveat—no serious 'soldering'!)

Jim is a member of Barnsley and Huddersfield RMSes, and is available to do music talks: please enquire via the FRMS secretary.
