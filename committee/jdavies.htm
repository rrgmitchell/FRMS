Committee member profile

John Davies, Chairman of FRMS

John was formerly Senior Lecturer at a Tertiary College. For him music has always been the antidote. He is currently the Secretary at South Cheshire RMS who meet in Nantwich. With others in the former North Mercia region he has taken steps to rebuild the region with the organisation of trips to concerts and other joint enterprises. He enjoys most types of music including jazz, classical and romantic, but has a special interest in twentieth century tonal music particularly Prokofiev, Shostakovich, Stravinsky, Bartok and Nielsen.
