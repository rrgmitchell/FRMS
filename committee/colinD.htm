Committee Member Profile

Armed with a chemistry degree from Exeter University I embarked on two years National Service where my training in electronics was useful for my future career.

I was lured into the battery industry by the exciting prospect that battery operated cars were no more than 10 years away. I retired some 40 years later when battery operated cars were no more than 10 years away.

My leisure interests are music, photography and walking, but lately arthritis has made walking less attractive and I have taken up the gentler excercise of lawn bowls.
I'm secretary of Cardiff RMS, competition secretary of my bowls club and webmaster for Newport Indoor Bowls Association.

Now that I'm on the committee of the FRMS, something's gotta give!
