Committee Member Profile

London born, Roger Apps has been involved for forty years in Recorded Music Societies. He has been a member and Chairman / Secretary / Treasurer many times of Maidstone RMS 1968 -2001. He was a founder member of Tenterden GS 1969 and a founder member of The Dvorak Society of G.B. 1974

Member Bath RMS 2008 – Committee member Bradford-on-Avon RMS 2002 –

Other interests : Steam trains and family history. He lives in Trowbridge, Wiltshire with wife, June and retired in 2007 after 44 years in administration work.

Music specialities: Scandinavian, French, East European and rare English works – orchestral, chamber, instrumental and choral, ( not opera ).
