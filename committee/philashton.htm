Committee Member Profile

Philip Ashton Eng Tech MIET - Technical Officer

Joined RAF at the tender age of 17 yrs. As an Aircraft Apprentice in the trade of “Air Radio Fitter” (today it would be called “technician).

After completing 12 yrs service, which was spent in the main at an experimental unit on trials of various guided weapons and associated test equipment. On completion of my contract I entered the world of automatic test equipment and subsequently main frame computers/pc’s etc.. My interest in electronics was not confined to computers as I was into HIFI and naturally reproduction of music recordings at the highest level. I made my own amplifiers and other equipment including a reel to reel tape recorder, radio tuners etc.

I became Chairman of the Hitchin RMS for some years until its demise due to lack of members. Although it is not completely dead as it is now reincarnated as ”Benslow Recorded Music for All”.

Currently I am the Vice Chairman of Luton U3A (possibly Chair in May) and run a Chamber Music Group which may be the only one in the UK U3A network.

I am a member of “The Institute of Incorporated Engineers” , a radio amateur, (call sign G0DCS) and finally a graduate of the of “The Institute of Supervisory Management”
