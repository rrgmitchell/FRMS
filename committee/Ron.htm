Ron Mitchell
WebMaster
Secretary of Leicester Music Society

Ron is an Italian-Scot from Edinburgh. After a degree in mathematics he taught secondary school for a while before moving into IT and now works as a programmer for a large international business services company. His musical skills include piano (grade 6), guitar (amateur folk) and tin whistle. Last year he joined a small choir, Sing For Change Coventry, currently meeting and singing by Zoom call.

Music Choices - Unashamedly late Romantic and early 20th century: Brahms, Rachmaninoff, Sibelius, Elgar, Nielsen... Great pianists of past and present. Scots, Irish and English folk song. And JS Bach: he reckons he is about 2.22% of the way through the 222-CD Complete Bach Edition of 2018.
