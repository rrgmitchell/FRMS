Torbay Musical Weekend 2025

Why not chase away the early winter blues with a visit to the seaside and the chance to hear live music and interesting talks in the company of fellow music enthusiasts?

The 2025 Torbay Musical Weekend will be held at the Grand Hotel, Torquay from Friday 14th to Monday 17th November 2025. Stay for two nights until Sunday lunchtime, or take in the full weekend until Monday breakfast.

This year's weekend will feature three concerts with Piers Adams and David Wright of baroque ensemble Red Priest and the Primrose Piano Quartet, plus guests. Together with a surrounding programme of talks and presentations on topics ranging from early keyboards to writing for film and media. Flexible residential packages are available.

More details at the new website: torbaymusicalweekend.co.uk
