Torbay Musical Weekend 2026

Why not chase away the early winter blues with a visit to the seaside and the chance to hear live music and interesting talks in the company of fellow music enthusiasts?

The 2026 Torbay Musical Weekend will be held at the Grand Hotel, Torquay from Friday 13th to Monday 16th November 2026. Stay for two nights until Sunday lunchtime, or take in the full weekend until Monday breakfast.
