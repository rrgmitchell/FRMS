Torbay Musical Weekend 2024

Why not chase away the early winter blues with a visit to the seaside and the chance to hear interesting talks and live music in the company of fellow music enthusiasts?

The 2024 Torbay Musical Weekend will be held at the Grand Hotel, Torquay from Friday 15th to Monday 18th November 2024. Stay for two nights until Sunday lunchtime, or take in the full weekend until Monday breakfast. See the leaflet below for full details.
