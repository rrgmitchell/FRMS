Dear Music Lover,

It is my pleasure to invite you to the FRMS Music Weekend 2023, which is to be held from Friday evening to Sunday afternoon 14th-16th April at the Derby Conference Centre, London Road, Derby. We hope that this new venue will enable attendance from all parts of the country since Derby is conveniently situated at the junction of major rail routes and close to the M1, A38 and A50 roads. The Conference Centre was formerly the LMS, and later British Rail, School of Transport. Now privately run, it has been modernised as a fully equipped conference centre and hotel.

The programme has still to be finalised but we are pleased to announce that presenters will include Norman Lebrecht, John Pickard, Robert Quinney, and Nigel Simeone. Presentations from within the FRMS will feature our Vice-chairman George Steele on George Gershwin and FRMS Treasurer Paul Corfield Godfrey on settings of Oscar Wilde.

The booking procedure is similar to that for the 2022 event in that delegates book rooms directly with the Conference Centre, but for this event the Centre will charge only for bed and breakfast, with day charges and dinner on both evenings being included in your conference fee. The total cost of the weekend, however, will be comparable to that in 2022, which was held at the original 2020 price. Please see the attached form for details of the booking procedure.

Early booking is advised. The Conference Centre will reserve accommodation for us until 3rd March after which bookings will be accepted on a first come first served basis.

If you are receiving this on behalf of a society please make this information known to members of your society.

I look forward to welcoming you to Derby in April.

Yours sincerely

Allan Child
FRMS Chairman and Weekend administrator.
