First Daventry 'Stars' announced

Patrick McCarthy, looking back over a career in music which included a surprise debut at the Proms in 1974.

This year's LIVE music - a song recital by young Swedish baritone Adam Jondelius from the Royal Welsh College of Music and Drama, which will include the world première of songs by Welsh composer Paul Corfield Godfrey.

Further details of programme and booking arrangements will be sent out in early January. If you would like to receive these and are not already on our mailing list please send your name and contact details by e-mail to secretary@thefrms.co.uk
