FRMS AGM 2024

First Mailing, 28-08-2024

The AGM will take place over Zoom on Saturday 2nd November. To attend remotely, complete the form in the invitation letter below and return it to the Secretary.

As explained in the letter, the Federation is in pressing need of more committee members to carry out its work - which is all done remotely over email and Zoom. If you'd like to help out, please complete and return the nomination form below.

FRMS AGM 2023

Last year's AGM was held remotely over Zoom.

Outgoing Chairman Allan Child handed over the reins to our new Chairman, Tony Sanderson. Allan has accepted the office of Vice-President of the Federation.

Former newsletter editor Simon Rawlings stepped down from the Committee. There are therefore four vacant positions on the Committee: anyone interested in joining the Committee please apply to the Secretary.
