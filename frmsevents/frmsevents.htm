FRMS AGM 2023

Last year's AGM was held remotely over Zoom.

Outgoing Chairman Allan Child handed over the reins to our new Chairman, Tony Sanderson. Allan has accepted the office of Vice-President of the Federation.

Former newsletter editor Simon Rawlings stepped down from the Committee. There are therefore four vacant positions on the Committee: anyone interested in joining the Committee please apply to the Secretary.
