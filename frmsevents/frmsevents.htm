April 2023 FRMS Music Weekend - cancelled

The 2023 Music Weekend, which was to have been held at the Derby Conference Centre from Friday 14th April to Sunday 16th April, has been cancelled. There were insufficient bookings to make it financially viable, or to make up an audience worthy of the time and trouble of our visiting speakers.

Now that the this year's Federation AGM will itself be a Zoom-only meeting, it seems unlikely, in the present climate at least, that there will be any future FRMS in-person events.
